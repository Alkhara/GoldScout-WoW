# GoldScout Contributor Terms

*Last updated: November 7, 2026*

These terms apply when you share drop data with GoldScout, for example by
submitting your `/gscout export` through the "Share drop data" issue form. They
are between you and the GoldScout maintainer (GitHub user Alkhara, "we" below).
By ticking the box on the form you agree to them.

## What you're sharing

A GoldScout export contains, for each creature you looted: its ID, name and
level range, how many times you killed or pickpocketed it, the items it dropped
and how often, and the average map position where you looted it. It contains no
character names, account names or other personal details. Your submission is
posted publicly on GitHub, together with your GitHub username. See the
[Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md) for details.

## What you promise

- You collected the data yourself by playing World of Warcraft with GoldScout.
- You didn't copy it from Wowhead or any other website, database, addon data

## How we handle submissions

- An automated check validates every submission, and we may review it by hand.
  We can accept, reject, adjust (for example, cap the weight of a single

- Combined data from many players is what ships. We don't promise that your

  reuse it. Your own copy of your data stays yours to use as you like.

## Removal

You can ask us to stop using your future submissions, or to remove your
submission file from the repository, by opening an issue. Data that was already
combined into a release or published data set may stay there, since it can't be
separated from other players' data.
