**What should *this* character be doing for gold right now?** GoldScout answers that for whichever character you're logged in on: its level, class, faction and professions, priced against your own auction house.

> **Get the most out of GoldScout: add GoldScout Companion.** The companion app for Windows and Mac brings auction house prices into GoldScout every hour, without scanning, and shares your drop data automatically so the Farm tab keeps getting better. Get it from the GoldScout website (link at the bottom of this page).

### Live prices, two ways
- **Every hour, without scanning:** with GoldScout Companion running, GoldScout gets Blizzard's hourly auction house snapshot for your realm and faction, plus the Blackwater (goblin) auction house as an extra tooltip line.
- **Right now, with one click:** press **Scan AH** at the auction house and GoldScout reads every item's lowest price and quantity in a few seconds. Your own scan always wins when it's newer.

Prices are kept per realm and faction and show on item tooltips. Until you have your own, GoldScout starts with regional estimates from Blizzard's data that ship with every release, so the Farm list works the moment you install it.

### Farm tab
Farms ranked by estimated gold per hour for your character:
- mobs with valuable drops, using real drop rates from players
- cloth from humanoids up to your level
- herbs, ore and leather your Herbalism, Mining or Skinning skill can gather
- fishing, zone by zone, for open water and each kind of fishing pool, from what players actually fish up
- valuable crafting reagents, the mob-dropped materials professions always need, selling on your auction house right now, even before anyone has learned where they drop

Three views:
- **By item:** one row per item, at its best source; expand a row to see every mob that drops it.
- **By mob:** one row per mob, with everything worth farming that it drops added up, vendor trash included.
- **Data wanted:** mobs near your level that need more kills before their drop rates are solid. Hunt one and share your loot, and everyone's rates get better.

The **Lvl** column is green when the mobs still give you experience, yellow when only some do and gray when none do. Tick **Gives XP** to level and earn at the same time.

### Every drop from any mob
Target a mob, right-click its portrait and choose **Drop rates** (or type `/gscout drops`). A window lists everything GoldScout players have looted from it, your own loot included: each item's drop chance, what it's worth and what it adds per kill, with pickpocketing listed separately.

Hovering a mob shows its best drops in the tooltip, how many times players have looted it, and what its loot is worth per kill at your auction house's prices.

### Craft tab
Every recipe you know, ranked by profit per craft: auction house price after the cut, minus the cheapest cost of the materials. Vendor prices are learned automatically.

### Hunt mode
Pick a farm and press **Hunt**:
- Blizzard's own waypoint arrow to where players find those mobs
- the hunting area shaded on the world map, from where you and other players have looted them
- an icon over the mobs you're hunting (turn on enemy nameplates)
- a live panel: kills, drops, your drop rate, and what you're earning, split into the item you're hunting, other loot and coin, with gold per hour for each
- a summary and history when you stop

### Gets smarter the more people play
Every corpse you loot teaches GoldScout what that mob really drops, on all your characters. Share what you've learned and it goes into the next day's release for everyone. The more players share, the better everyone's Farm tab gets, and everyone who shares is ranked on the contributor leaderboard.

**GoldScout Companion** shares for you: it runs quietly in the system tray, never shows pop-ups over your game, and uploads new data after you agree once. It only reads GoldScout's saved data and writes one small data addon; it never touches the running game. Without it, `/gscout export` gives you your data to paste into the share page on the GoldScout website.

### Honest data
Every drop rate and spawn point comes from GoldScout players looting in game. Nothing is copied from Wowhead or any other site.

### Plays by the rules
GoldScout only shows information. It never moves, targets, attacks, loots or marks anything for you, and it respects every limit Blizzard puts on addons. Every in-game feature is the same for everyone.
